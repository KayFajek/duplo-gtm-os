# Duplo brand voice

Source: `context/docs/research/voice.md` unless tagged otherwise (checked 2026-11-12). The tone traits below are INFERRED from Duplo's own copy. There are no formal brand guidelines in the research.

## Tone of voice

| Trait | What it looks like in Duplo's copy |
|---|---|
| Plain and benefit-led | "Automate and track expenses effortlessly" |
| Short headline fragments | "Built on security." / "Driven by compliance." |
| Occasionally conversational | "Ditch the manual approval process." |
| Confident about scale and reach | "send and receive payments in 80+ currencies across 160+ countries" [company] |
| Compliance as reassurance, not fear | "Stay compliant, audit-ready, and disruption-free" |
| Pan-African, with global reach | "We are on a mission to power commerce across the continent." |

## Style rules
- Product names: Expense Management, Global Payments, Global Accounts, NRS E-invoicing, Treasury Management. The payments API is "Atlas" [company].
- Duplo uses "&" in some names ("Approval & Controls API") [company].
- Call the tax authority "NRS" consistently. Duplo's own site mixes NRS, FIRS and NIRS [company].
- Calls to action Duplo already uses: "Book a Demo", "See Duplo in action", "Sign up for free".
- No em dashes (house rule, `CLAUDE.md`).
