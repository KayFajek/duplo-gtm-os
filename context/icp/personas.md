# Duplo buyer personas

Built only from `context/docs/research/` (checked 2026-11-12). Tags: [company] [customers] [competitors] [market] [signals] [voice].
Big caveat: the research has almost no buyer quotes. The only customer words are from Prunet Digital and PressOne. Anywhere a pain is shown in Duplo's own copy rather than the buyer's words, it is labelled **(Duplo's framing)**. INFERRED = our reading, not a stated fact.

## 1. Champion: finance operations lead

|---|---|
| Job titles | Operations Lead, Operations Associate, Regional Operations Manager, Treasury Manager (all titles from Duplo testimonials) [customers]. AP Officer and Treasury Analyst (INFERRED) [signals] |

What success sounds like: "Duplo gave us our time back." (Prunet Digital) [customers]

## 2. Economic buyer: CEO or finance head

| | |
|---|---|
| Job titles | CEO and Founder & CEO (Duplo testimonials; at Prunet, the CEO found Duplo) [customers]. CFO (Duplo blog targets CFOs) [voice]. Head of Finance and Financial Controller (INFERRED) [signals] |
| Measured on | Not found. INFERRED: tax compliance exposure, payment cost and cash visibility, based on what Duplo leads with [market] [company] |
| What makes them start looking | The e-invoicing deadline for their turnover band (medium: enforcement from January 2027). Penalty exposure. Cash spread across banks or entities. A new finance leader (INFERRED) [market] [signals] |

Top 3 pains. None are in the buyer's own words.
1. Penalty exposure: ₦200,000 plus 100% of the tax due for bypassing e-invoicing [market]. **(Market fact, not a quote)**
2. Cash scattered "across every bank, account and entity" with no real-time position **(Duplo's framing)** [voice] [customers]
3. Spreadsheet-run spend: "Why CFOs Are Moving Away from Spreadsheets for Spend Management" **(Duplo's framing)** [voice]

Objections. The questions Duplo's FAQ answers suggest these (INFERRED):
- "Can I continue using PDF invoices?" (Duplo's answer: "No.") [market]
- Price. The pricing page shows two structures and doesn't say which applies [company].
- Vendor choice. At least nine other SI and APP providers are accredited, including Interswitch and DigiTax [competitors].

## 3. Influencer: tax and systems owner

| | |
|---|---|
| Job titles | Tax or VAT Officer (INFERRED) [signals]. IT or ERP owner (INFERRED: compliance requires connecting systems through an SI or APP) [market]. Engineering or product lead for API buyers such as Kredete and Riz Remit (INFERRED) [customers] |
| Measured on | Not found |
| What makes them start looking | ERP tax-code remapping for the 2026 tax reform. A buyer rejecting invoices without an IRN. Their segment's e-invoicing go-live [market] [signals] |

Top 3 pains. None are in the buyer's own words.
1. Integrating SAP, Oracle, Dynamics or QuickBooks with the NRS platform through an SI or APP. Advisers also recommend remapping ERP tax codes for the new WHT and VAT rates [market] [company]
2. Staying "audit-ready" **(Duplo's framing)** [voice]
3. Losing input-VAT credit on invoices that weren't cleared [market]. **(Market fact, not a quote)**

Objections (INFERRED):
- Security and data. Duplo shows ISO 27001 and 22301, PCI DSS and NDPR badges to answer this [company].
- Integration depth with their specific ERP [company].
- Credibility. Duplo's site calls the authority NRS, FIRS and "NIRS" on different pages [company].
