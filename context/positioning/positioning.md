## Positioning statement

For businesses operating in Nigeria whose finance teams pay, collect and now clear invoices across many suppliers, stores and banks, Duplo is the financial operating system that runs payments, spend, NRS e-invoicing and treasury on one platform. Today those teams work across banking apps, spreadsheets, WhatsApp and separate tools [customers] [competitors]. Duplo is licensed by the NRS as both Systems Integrator and Access Point Provider [company], so an invoice can be cleared, paid and reconciled in the same system. Maersk, Heineken and Eat'N'Go already use it [customers].
