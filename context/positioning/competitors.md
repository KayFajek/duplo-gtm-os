# Duplo competitors

Source: `context/docs/research/competitors.md` unless tagged otherwise (checked 2026-11-12). Other tags: [company] [customers] [market] [voice].
INFERRED = our reading. The research holds no third-party head-to-head comparison and no win/loss data, so every "wins", "loses" and "respond" line below is INFERRED from the facts cited.

## Main competitors

| Competitor | Type | How it positions itself | Overlap with Duplo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bujeti | Spend and finance-ops platform, Nigeria and Kenya, YC-backed | "Control your business finances, all in one place". Products: corporate cards, expense, payments, invoicing, tax, payroll, multi-currency. "Join over 1,000 businesses" | Same "one platform" pitch, same buyer |
| Flex Finance | Spend management, Nigeria | Corporate cards, approval workflows, expense tracking, vendor payments. Quote-based pricing. Website unreachable on 2026-11-12 | Spend and approvals |
| Paystack MFB (absorbing Brass) | Business banking | Brass customers move to Paystack MFB before 2026-07-31. Expense controls and reporting | SMB spend |
| Moniepoint (Moniebook) | MSME POS and bookkeeping | POS, inventory, staff, bookkeeping. Business expense card | Small-business end only |
| Interswitch, Redtech, DigiTax, Afri Invoice, Pillarcraft, Pronalytics, Remita | NRS e-invoicing providers (SI and/or APP) | Accredited to connect businesses to the NRS platform | E-invoicing |
| Passpoint, Nomba, ZitraPay, Oneremit, XTransfer | Cross-border payments and treasury | FX, payouts, multi-currency treasury (vendor claims) | Global payments, treasury |
| Banks | Business banking | Duplo frames banks as distribution: "Banks as Distribution. Fintechs as Infrastructure." [company] | Channel and competitor |

## Where Duplo wins (INFERRED)
- **E-invoicing plus payments.** Duplo holds both NRS licences and settles cleared invoices in the same system [company]. No spend tool in the research is shown to hold NRS licences, and the e-invoicing providers are not shown running payments or spend.
- **Enterprise proof.** Named users include Maersk, Heineken, DP World and Krones [customers]. Bujeti's named testimonials are Selar, AutoGirl and Koolboks.
- **Multi-location operators.** Eat'N'Go runs 70+ stores on Duplo [customers].
- **Distribution through banks.** Wema Bank embeds Duplo in ALAT Business [company].

## Where Duplo loses (INFERRED)
- **Corporate cards and payroll.** Bujeti and Flex Finance sell corporate cards, and Bujeti sells payroll. Duplo's site lists neither [company].
- **Published ROI.** Bujeti claims "Positive customer ROI in 5 months, reaching 4.5x within 3 years." Duplo has no equivalent figure in the research.
- **Pricing clarity.** Duplo's pricing page shows two unrelated pricing structures [company].
- **Small businesses wanting POS or bookkeeping.** That is Moniebook's territory.
- **South Africa.** CEO: "some gaps in our regulatory framework there" [market]. Duplo relies on Ozow there [company].

## How to respond (INFERRED)

| When the prospect says | Respond with |
|---|---|
| "We're looking at Bujeti" or another spend tool | Ask how they will handle NRS e-invoicing. Duplo covers spend and compliant invoicing in one system [company] |
| "We already have an e-invoicing provider" | Duplo also pays and reconciles the cleared invoice ("Compliance-to-Commerce") [company] |
| "We need corporate cards" | Not a Duplo product in the research. Qualify out, or confirm with Duplo before claiming it |
| "Our bank does this" | Duplo works "without replacing your existing banks or ERP" [voice], and Wema Bank distributes it [company] |
| "We're a small shop and need POS" | Not a fit (see `context/icp/icp.md`) |
