# Duplo value proposition by persona

Source: `context/docs/research/` only (checked 2026-11-12).
Tags name the research file each claim comes from: [company] [customers] [competitors] [market] [signals] [voice].
- "Duplo claim" = Duplo's own, unverified figure.
- INFERRED = our reading, not stated in the research.

Personas are the three defined in `context/icp/personas.md`.

---

## Champion: finance operations lead
Titles: Operations Lead, Operations Associate, Regional Operations Manager, Treasury Manager (Duplo testimonial titles) [customers].

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Get payments approved, paid and reconciled without chasing people across tools. INFERRED from Duplo's expense flow: submit, set budgets, approve, track [voice] |
| **Pain** | "Managing the money side of this was absolute chaos if I'm being honest." (Nma, Operations Lead, Prunet Digital) [customers]<br>Finance run on banking apps, spreadsheets, WhatsApp and email [customers]<br>"So many payments. So many tools. Too many approvals stuck in a queue." (Duplo's framing) [customers] |
| **Gain** | Custom approval workflows. Bulk payments to "up to 500 recipients at a time". Auto reconciliation [company]<br>"Duplo gave us our time back." (Prunet Digital) [customers] |
| **Proof** | Prunet Digital moved expense requests off WhatsApp into approval workflows and pays salaries by spreadsheet upload [customers]<br>Eat'N'Go runs over 70 of its 200+ stores on Duplo [customers]<br>Duplo claim: payment costs cut "by up to 85%" [customers] |

## Economic buyer: CEO or finance head
Titles: CEO, Founder & CEO (testimonial titles) [customers]. CFO (Duplo's blog targets CFOs) [voice].

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Stay compliant and see where money sits and goes across the business. INFERRED from what Duplo leads with: e-invoicing, spend visibility, treasury [company] |
| **Pain** | Penalty exposure for bypassing e-invoicing: ₦200,000 plus 100% of the tax due, plus interest [market]<br>Cash spread across banks, accounts and entities, with manual consolidation and no real-time cash position [customers]<br>Spend management on spreadsheets: Duplo blog "Why CFOs Are Moving Away from Spreadsheets for Spend Management" [voice] |
| **Gain** | One platform for collections, payouts, FX, reconciliation and e-invoicing. CEO: "the single operational layer that finally connects payments, invoicing and tax logic." [voice]<br>Treasury that works "without replacing your existing banks or ERP." [voice] |
| **Proof** | Duplo holds both NRS licences: Systems Integrator and Access Point Provider [company]<br>Named users: Maersk, Krones, DP World, Baobab, Miva Open University, Eat'N'Go, Heineken [customers]<br>Duplo claim: "more than 1,000 businesses" and "over $1 billion in annualised payment volume" [customers] |

## Influencer: tax and systems owner
Titles: Tax or VAT officer, ERP or IT owner (INFERRED from what compliance requires) [market].

| | |
|---|---|
| **Job to be done** | Get every invoice cleared by the NRS from the systems already in place, and pass audit. INFERRED from the compliance steps [market] |
| **Pain** | Compliance means onboarding to the NRS platform, integrating "through approved Access Point Providers or Systems Integrators", testing and transmitting [market]<br>An invoice without clearance "can cost you (and potentially your buyer) input-VAT credit." [market]<br>PDF invoices are no longer accepted [market] |
| **Gain** | "Stay compliant, audit-ready, and disruption-free" [voice]<br>Approved invoices carry an Invoice Reference Number (IRN), a cryptographic stamp and a QR code [company]<br>ERP integrations: SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Microsoft Dynamics, with UBL/XML output [company] |
| **Proof** | NRS SI and APP licences [company]<br>"ISO 27001 & 22301", "PCI DSS Compliant", "NDPR Compliant" [company] |
