### Product history
- 2022: started with collections into virtual accounts for FMCG distributors and their retailers, plus payouts, reconciliation, invoicing, bill approval, ERP sync. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/31/duplo-digitizes-payment-flows-for-african-b2b-enterprises-gets-4-3m-seed-funding [PRESS, 2022-08-31]
- 2022 pre-seed coverage also mentions credit to business customers and embedded payments / BNPL for marketplaces. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2022/02/10/yc-backed-duplo-raises-1-3m-pre-seed-to-build-financial-os-for-b2b-companies-in-nigeria [PRESS, 2022-02-10]
- INFERRED: the company has moved from a collections tool for FMCG distributors toward a broad finance-ops suite (spend, payables, e-invoicing, treasury) aimed at mid-market and enterprise.

## Pricing model

The pricing page shows two separate structures and does not say how they relate. Source: https://www.tryduplo.com/pricing [SITE]

Structure A (no prices shown): Essentials ("Automate your basic financial operations"), Pro (badge "Popular"; "Manage your financial operations at scale"), Enterprise ("Custom requests for large teams"). CTAs "Book a Demo" / "Request a Quote". All three include "100 Free Payouts (Bank Transfer)", "Unlimited Recipients and Suppliers", "Vendor Portal". Essentials caps approvals at "maximum of 3 approval stages"; Pro has "Complex Approvals (unlimited stages)" and "Automatic payment reconciliation".

Structure B ("Transparent Pricing - No Hidden Cost"):
| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Payout ₦5,000 and below | ₦10 per transaction |
| Payout ₦5,001 to ₦50,000 | ₦25 per transaction |
| Payout ₦50,001 and above | ₦50 per transaction |
| Free Tier | $0/month, 0.5% on collections |
| Premium Tier 1 | $50/month, 0.3% on collections |
| Premium Tier 2 | $100/month, 0.1% on collections |
| PSP Tier | Custom, bulk discounts, white-label |

2022 pricing (historical): 1% fee per transaction; virtual account creation ₦100 to ₦1,000. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2022/02/10/yc-backed-duplo-raises-1-3m-pre-seed-to-build-financial-os-for-b2b-companies-in-nigeria [PRESS]

Wema Bank channel: three months free for existing ALAT Business customers, then Duplo and Wema share subscription revenue (split not disclosed); Duplo also earns transaction fees. Source: https://www.brandiconimage.com/2026/07/duplo-expands-growth-strategy-with-wema.html [PRESS reprint of TechCabal reporting, URL dated 2026-07]

INFERRED: revenue is a mix of SaaS subscription and per-transaction fees.
