## C. Company events that match Duplo's customer stories

### Multi-location growth / store expansion
- Evidence: Eat'N'Go case study: "over 200 stores, thousands of employees, and dozens of vendor relationships"; MDS: 44 locations. Sources: https://tryduplo.com/blog/from-pizza-to-payments-how-eatngo-is-powering-their-finance-team-with-duplo/ [SITE]; https://businessday.ng/companies/article/nigeria-must-tap-opportunities-in-africas-b2b-payments-oyekola/ [PRESS]
- INFERRED signal: new branch / store openings, regional expansion announcements.

### Many vendors, many payouts
- Evidence: bulk payments "Pay up to 500 recipients at a time"; blog "Bulk Payments for Businesses in Nigeria: How to Pay Vendors 10x Faster". Sources: https://www.tryduplo.com/; https://tryduplo.com/case-studies/ [SITE]
- INFERRED signal: large distributor/retailer networks, contractor-heavy firms (security companies, logistics), churches with many branches (Duplo blog targets these).

### Approvals running on WhatsApp / spreadsheets
- Evidence: Prunet: expense requests on WhatsApp; "Managing the money side of this was absolute chaos if I'm being honest." Source: https://tryduplo.com/blog/duplo-customer-spotlight-prunet-digital-marketing-agency/ [SITE]
- INFERRED signal: hiring posts for finance/admin roles that mention manual reconciliation, Excel, or "payment processing" duties.

### Paying or collecting across borders
- Evidence: Riz Remit funds GBP and disburses in NGN; blog on professional services firms "Billing International Clients and Paying Overseas Contractors". Sources: https://tryduplo.com/payments/; https://www.tryduplo.com/blog [SITE]
- INFERRED signal: Nigerian firms with foreign clients or suppliers, new international office, foreign investor.

### Cash across many banks and entities
- Evidence: TMS pitch: cash spread across banks, accounts and entities, manual consolidation; "Several major FMCG businesses are currently exploring Duplo TMS". Source: https://tryduplo.com/blog/treasury-management-system-nigeria/ [SITE]
- INFERRED signal: group restructures, new subsidiaries, acquisitions, new treasury manager or treasurer hire.
