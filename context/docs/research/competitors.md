# Duplo: competitors research (raw)

Checked: 2026-11-12. Raw research, not conclusions. Quotes are exact. "INFERRED" marks interpretation.
Source quality key: [SITE] company's own website, [PRESS] journalist article, [PR] press release, [AGG] aggregator, [SNIPPET] search summary only, page not opened.

Note: no source found that directly compares Duplo with competitors. Grouping by category below is INFERRED from Duplo's product lines (spend management, payments, e-invoicing, treasury).

## 1. Spend and finance-ops platforms (Nigeria)

### Bujeti
- Headline: "Control your business finances, all in one place"
- Subheadline (opening): "Bujeti brings your entire financial operation into one unified platform,"
- Products: Expense management, Corporate cards, Payments automation, Bank connect, Invoicing, Tax management, Payroll, Multi-currency, Bujeti app; also Budget management, Approval workflows, Policy management, Vendor management, "Bujeti Intelligence" (BRAIN).
- Targets: "Startups", "Mid-sized Businesses", "Large Businesses", "Enterprises". Tagline "Built for Africans by Africans".
- Proof: "Join over 1,000 businesses"; "Over 1,000 CFOs, controllers, accountants, and finance admins". Testimonials from Selar (Seyi Ogunbiyi, COO), AutoGirl (Chichi Arinze, CEO & Co-founder), Koolboks (Abiodun Olamilekan).
- ROI claim: "Positive customer ROI in 5 months, reaching 4.5x within 3 years."
- Regulatory footer: "Bujeti is a technology company, not a bank. Banking services are provided by our licensed partner banks, regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria."
- Integrations: QuickBooks, Slack, Zoho.
Source: https://www.bujeti.com/ [SITE]
- Founded 2022, YC-backed, $2M seed (per search summary); "over 5,000 finance professionals across Nigeria and Kenya"; launched a tax management tool. Sources: https://geo.sig.ai/brands/bujeti.md [AGG, SNIPPET]; https://thecondia.com/partners/nigerian-fintech-bujeti-tax-compliance-tool/ [SNIPPET]
- INFERRED: the closest like-for-like competitor (same YC lineage, same "one platform" pitch, same buyer). Bujeti leads with corporate cards and payroll; Duplo's site does not list corporate cards or payroll as products.

### Flex Finance
- Described as a Nigerian spend management platform with physical and virtual corporate cards, approval workflows, expense tracking, reimbursements and vendor payments; quote-based pricing. Sources: https://disrupt-africa.com/2023/06/30/how-nigerias-flex-finance-controls-spend-management-for-businesses-in-africa/ [SNIPPET]; https://www.getvergo.com/learn/flex-finance-alternatives [SNIPPET]
- Own website: Not found (withflex.co DNS error; flexfinance.co HTTP 525 on 2026-11-12). Current operating status: Not found.

### Brass (now part of Paystack MFB)
- Brass business banking (accounts, payroll, expense management) is being folded into Paystack Microfinance Bank; customers moved before 2026-07-31. Sources: https://techcabal.com/2026/06/01/brass-moves-to-paystack-mfb/ [SNIPPET]; https://tech.africa/brass-paystack-mfb/ [SNIPPET]
- Paystack says it already offers "expense controls, transaction monitoring, and financial reporting tools" (search summary wording, not verified on Paystack's site; paystack.com returned 403).
- INFERRED: Paystack MFB becomes a business-banking competitor for SMB spend; Brass customers in transition are a possible displacement pool.

### Moniepoint (Moniebook)
- Moniebook launched December 2025: POS, inventory, staff management, branch oversight, bookkeeping, receipts. Business page advertises an expense card. Sources: https://weetracker.com/2025/12/03/moniepoint-launches-moniebook-nigeria-bookkeeping-bet/ [SNIPPET]; https://moniepoint.com/ng/business [SNIPPET]
- INFERRED: overlaps mainly at the MSME end, not the mid-market AP / treasury buyer.

### Others named in passing

## 4. Banks
- Banks themselves are both a channel and a competitor. Duplo's Wema deal frames banks as distribution: blog title "Banks as Distribution. Fintechs as Infrastructure." Source: https://www.tryduplo.com/blog [SITE]
- CEO: banks are asked for services beyond their offerings, and "Doing that work as a bank would require layers of approvals and technical resources." Source: https://www.brandiconimage.com/2026/07/duplo-expands-growth-strategy-with-wema.html [PRESS]
