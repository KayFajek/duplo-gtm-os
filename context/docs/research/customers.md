Checked: 2026-11-12. Raw research, not conclusions. Quotes are exact. "INFERRED" marks interpretation.
Source quality key: [SITE] Duplo website, [PRESS] journalist article, [PR] press release or reprint, [SNIPPET] search summary only, page not opened.

## Industries served
- Early customers: FMCG distributors, live from January 2022. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/31/duplo-digitizes-payment-flows-for-african-b2b-enterprises-gets-4-3m-seed-funding [PRESS]
- Expanded to "B2B logistics, medical supplies" (CEO). Source: https://fintech-intel.com/boardroom-talk/duplo-co-founder-yele-oyekola-on-digitising-payments-in-africa/ [SNIPPET]
- Payments page target sectors: "Marketplaces, SaaS & Digital Platforms, Fintechs & Payment Platforms, Cross-Border Businesses, E-commerce, Education, B2B Commerce, Logistics, Healthcare". Source: https://tryduplo.com/payments/ [SITE]
- Blog content targets: churches, professional services firms, security companies, FMCG. Source: https://www.tryduplo.com/blog and https://tryduplo.com/case-studies/ [SITE]
- South Africa: "retail, professional services, logistics, construction, and financial services" (no names). Source: https://tryduplo.com/blog/duplo-south-africa-payments-expenses-reconciliation/ [SITE]
- Treasury: "Several major FMCG businesses are currently exploring Duplo TMS". Source: https://tryduplo.com/blog/treasury-management-system-nigeria/ [SITE]

## Company sizes
- Pricing tiers span free to Enterprise. Source: https://www.tryduplo.com/pricing [SITE]
- Named logos range from a Lagos agency (Prunet) to multinationals (Maersk, Heineken, DP World).
- INFERRED: buyers range from SMBs (self-serve free tier) to enterprises, with volume concentrated in the roughly 20% mid-market and enterprise segment.
