## Recurring words and themes (INFERRED from the quotes above)
- "one platform" / "unified" / "single operational layer": consolidation is the core promise.
- "automate", "effortlessly", "simple", "without complex integrations": low effort.
- "compliant", "audit-ready", "secure", licences shown as badges: trust and regulation.
- "African businesses", "across the continent", "global": pan-African plus global ambition.
- Tone: plain, benefit-led, short headline fragments; occasional conversational lines ("Ditch the manual approval process."). Uses "&" in product names.
- Words Duplo uses for itself: "financial operating system", "payment infrastructure", "payment rail". It does not use "neobank" or "bank" for itself (INFERRED from pages read).
